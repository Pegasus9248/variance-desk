06

Scope probe

A tool that answers anything answers nothing reliably. Send it something off-topic and watch the refusal. The guard is in the system instruction and is also enforced after the fact by a keyword check.

Not financial advice. This is a coursework prototype for an AI applications module. It produces management-accounting commentary on figures you supply. It does not value a business, forecast, or recommend any security or course of action.

Where your data goes. The CSV is parsed and the variance arithmetic is run entirely in your browser. If you select a model engine, the computed variance table is transmitted to that provider: Anthropic for the in-page Claude engine, Google for Gemini. The rule engine transmits nothing. No input is stored on any server by this page, and nothing persists after you close the tab except your light or dark preference.

You are reading machine-written commentary. Every narrative in section 05 is generated, including the rule-engine output. Check the figure audit before quoting anything.
